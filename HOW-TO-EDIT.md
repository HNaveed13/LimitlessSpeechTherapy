# How to edit the Limitless website

You can change any of the words on the website yourself. No coding needed, and you
can't break the design: the layout, colours and fonts stay the same no matter what
you type.

## Getting in

1. Go to **your-website.com/keystatic** (add `/keystatic` to the end of the address).
2. Sign in with the account from your invitation email.

## Making a change

1. The menu on the left follows the homepage **from top to bottom**:
   - **Top of the page:** the big headline, the sentence under it, the button, and the four quick facts
   - **How we work:** the large statement and your six principles
   - **Services:** the service areas visitors can open and close
   - **How therapy works:** the three steps
   - **About Alyssa:** your bio, photo and credentials
   - **Contact section:** the heading and the note under the form

   **Contact details, insurance & search** holds your phone numbers, email, address,
   insurance plans, and how the site appears on Google.
2. Click a section, change the text, then click **Save**.
3. The live website updates in **about a minute**. Refresh the page to see it.

## Handy tips

- **Bold a few words:** put two asterisks on each side, like `**neurodiversity-affirming**`.
- **New paragraph:** leave an empty line between paragraphs.
- **Reorder** service areas, steps, principles or credentials by dragging them in the list.
- **Add or remove** items with the buttons at the bottom of each list.
- **Phone numbers:** the first number in the list is the one on the "Call" button at the
  top of the site. Every number appears in the contact section and footer.
- **Your photo:** under About Alyssa, upload a new one. A portrait (taller than it is
  wide) looks best. Please also update the short photo description; it's read aloud
  to visitors who use screen readers.
- **Keep it short:** the headline looks best under six words, and the sentence under
  it around twenty words.

## If something looks wrong

Every save is recorded, so any change can be undone. Contact your web person with
what you changed and roughly when, and they can put it back.
