How it works (01 – 03)

Services Designed Around the Individual

01

Comprehensive Evaluations

Comprehensive speech and language evaluations are designed to provide an in-depth understanding of an individual’s communication strengths and areas of need.

Assessments are individualized, play-based and client-centered when appropriate, and may include standardized testing, clinical observation, caregiver and teacher input, and evaluation of functional communication abilities using both formal and informal measures.

02

Individualized Intervention

Therapy is designed to ensure an individualized and functional approach that supports meaningful progress and promotes confidence and success in everyday communication.

Establishing strong rapport is a foundational element of effective speech and language therapy. When clients feel comfortable, understood, and respected, they are more likely to engage in sessions, take communication risks, and make meaningful progress toward their goals.

Incorporating a client’s interests is a key strategy for strengthening this connection. By using preferred topics, activities, and strengths within sessions, therapy becomes more engaging and personally relevant. This individualized approach not only enhances participation and attention but also fosters authentic communication and lasting skill development.

03

Collaborative Care

Meaningful progress happens when support extends beyond the therapy room.

Collaboration with families, schools, and related providers is a key component of effective speech and language intervention, ensuring consistency across environments and promoting progress toward individualized goals.

A strong emphasis is placed on working closely with families and caregivers by providing clear guidance, education, and strategies to support carryover into everyday routines. This team-based, coordinated approach helps align strategies, share progress, and support each individual’s success across home, school, and community settings.

About (03 / 04)

Alyssa LaPlaga M.S., CCC-SLP

At the heart of this practice is a commitment to providing compassionate, individualized speech and language therapy that empowers clients and families to build confidence, strengthen communication, and thrive in everyday life.

Alyssa has extensive experience supporting children and families across a wide range of communication needs, She utilizes a person-centered planning approach to ensure that therapy is guided by each client’s strengths, preferences, and meaningful life goals, with input from families and support systems.

Recognizing the importance of individualized care, Alyssa created her private practice to offer flexible, client-centered speech and language services. Through a compassionate and family-focused approach, she is committed to helping clients reach their fullest communication potential.

Extensive Training and Experience
