How we work

Great therapy starts with the person — their strengths, their interests, and the goals that matter in real life.

Current research, clinical expertise, and each family’s priorities come together to shape treatment plans with goals that reflect what matters most for everyday communication.

Sessions are play-based and client-led where it helps, creating natural opportunities to learn. And because no two people are the same, therapy stays flexible — evolving alongside each client’s growth so progress carries beyond the session and into daily life.

How it works

Three steps, designed around the individual

01

Comprehensive evaluation

An in-depth look at communication strengths and needs — using standardized testing, clinical observation, caregiver and teacher input, and functional measures. The results guide personalized, meaningful goals.

02

Individualized intervention

Therapy built on trust and real interests. Using preferred topics and activities makes sessions engaging and personally relevant — turning participation and attention into lasting progress.

03

Collaborative care

Progress that extends well beyond the therapy room. Close work with families, schools, and providers keeps strategies consistent across home, school, and community settings.

Meet your clinician

Alyssa LaPlaga, M.S., CCC-SLP

A licensed and certified speech-language pathologist committed to compassionate, individualized care that helps clients and families build confidence and thrive in everyday life.

Since 2014, Alyssa has practiced as a licensed and certified SLP across public-school and private-practice settings, supporting individuals across the lifespan — from birth through adulthood. She uses a person-centered approach, letting each client’s strengths, preferences, and meaningful goals lead the way, with input from families and support systems.

She created Limitless to offer flexible, client-centered care, and she treats continuing education as essential to providing high-quality, evidence-based therapy — continually refining her skills to meet each client’s evolving needs.
